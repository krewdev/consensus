# Charter v0.1 — Constitutional Consensus Cooperative

Public brand: Consensus.

Preamble. We map replacements for failing systems. We do not pick parties or candidates.

1. Purpose. Member-owned public-opinion lab. The product is the map of what people will accept as replacements, with tradeoffs attached.
2. Scope. Systems and mechanisms only. No candidate pages, no party rankings, no GOTV.
3. Membership. One person, one vote. Founder, donors, and staff get no extra policy vote.
4. Issue format. Problem mechanism, who pays today, at least three replacement cards. Each card: tradeoff, who pays, 90-day test, where tried.
5. Voting. Agree / disagree / pass, or rank 1/2/3. No reply-war as the product.
6. Honesty. Sample size always shown. Label results “this room, not the national electorate.”
7. Consensus rule. Publish as consensus only what survives across opposing clusters. Plurality is reported separately.
8. Amendment. Charter changes need a higher bar than ordinary issue votes. Post the text 7 days prior.
9. Money and speech. No endorsing, opposing, or donating to candidates or parties. No token. No voter file.
10. Capture. No party, PAC, or single donor sets the agenda. Agenda is member-ranked. Sources required on featured briefs.

Constitutional means a written operating charter for this room — not a side in a 1787 fight.
