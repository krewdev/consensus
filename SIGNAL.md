# Signal #0 — welcome email

Subject: You joined the room. Housing first.

---

You are on the Consensus list.

Consensus is a co-op that maps what people will accept as replacements for failing systems.

Not a party.
Not a candidate list.
Not the conference.
Not a chain.

Constitutional here means a written operating charter: who votes, what a proposal must include, how we measure agreement, how the rules change. Not a side in a 1787 fight.

This week the system is housing.

The problem mechanism: legal housing is slower and more expensive to add than demand. Renters and first-time buyers pay that delay.

Three replacements we are ranking:

1. By-right duplex / fourplex on ordinary lots.
   Tradeoff: neighborhood form changes. Who pays: mostly private builders.

2. Permit shot clock. If the city misses the deadline, the file moves.
   Tradeoff: less discretionary review. Who pays: staff capacity.

3. Incentive only after a unit is occupied, not after paper is entitled.
   Tradeoff: public revenue. Who pays: taxpayers.

Reply 1, 2, or 3. We will publish the tally as this room, not as America.

Sunday you get Signal #1: sample size, plurality, and any statement that survived across clusters.

If a post needs a name on a ballot to work, it does not ship.
